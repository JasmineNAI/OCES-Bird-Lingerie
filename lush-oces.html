SCENT IS A QUESTION

What does
attraction
feel like?

A fragrance can evoke a place for us. For a bird, information may travel through colour, movement, sound or scent in very different ways.

This collection helps fund the work of asking those questions carefully. OCES tests each idea against the perception and behaviour of the intended species.
