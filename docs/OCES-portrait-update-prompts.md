## Shared prompt brief

Edit the supplied photograph into a clean photographic cutout. Remove the wall and surrounding background entirely. Retain the person's face, expression, hair, glasses or jewellery, clothing, pose and proportions. Use neutral black-and-white photographic tones and preserve fabric and hair detail. Centre the person in a vertical frame with a small transparent margin. Include all visible torso through the source's bottom edge. Do not add text, objects, a coloured tint, a different outfit, beauty retouching or identity changes.
