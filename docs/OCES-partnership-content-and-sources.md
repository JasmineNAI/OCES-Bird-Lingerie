# OCES / LUSH × OCES · current partnership content

Updated 7 October 2026. This local academic website presents an authored fictional 2095 collaboration; it makes no claim about actual current Lush sourcing or sponsorship.

## Public narrative

The partnership funds Project EROS Phase II: scent, material interaction and species-specific courtship research. The public partnership page uses a polished, aspirational sponsor voice: care beyond an everyday ritual, bird-safe materials, protected avian food resources and sustained conservation support. It explicitly states that current materials in this fictional setting have no negative impact on birds, their food resources or their habitats.

The author’s hidden story about extraction and reputation repair remains background fiction and is not disclosed in the public partnership copy. Existing Bird Lingerie outcomes precede this partnership. Phase II has no published recovery or courtship-success results.
